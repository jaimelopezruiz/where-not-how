Fingertip coverage inside the contact window (T7 gate), per-episode table in finger_coverage.csv
(python -m extract.finger_coverage; image-space detections of the index fingertip, MediaPipe hand landmarker)

Rule: the contact window runs from the first to the last contact sample of the episode. A gap is a stretch of frames
without a detection, measured between the detections around it (or to the window edge when the detections start after
the window start or end before its end). Gaps of at most 0.5 s are bridged linearly between fingertip detections;
nothing is filled from the box. An episode is usable when no gap longer than 0.5 s remains in the window.
Stop rule: fewer than 4 of 7 usable test episodes ends T7 at this table.

Detector settings were chosen on 9 evenly spaced TRAIN episodes (ep_000 004 010 015 026 034 058 062 066) by mean
in-window coverage of the raw detections; the test split was not used to choose.

A 400 px crop around the box (the first idea) found no hand at all in ep_010 (0% against 84% for the full frame): the
crop cuts the hand off and the palm detector needs the hand, so the crop has to be large. Crops are scaled to 800 px.
Plausibility of what was detected (train subset, in-window detections): distance from the fingertip pixel to the box
centre, converted with the marker's pixel size: median 44-60 mm per episode (box half side 40.5 mm + tip radius), 93%
of detections within 30-80 mm at conf 0.1 (94% at conf 0.3), 2% beyond 120 mm; the implausible ones sit in ep_034 and
ep_058.

Sensitivity of the verdict (reported, not used): the test count is 3 because ep_005 has no fingertip detection for the
first 0.58 s of its contact window (the hand enters the frame late; no gap inside the push, longest 0.32 s). If gaps
at the two ends of the window were tolerated, as a replay that starts at the first sample and holds the last one
could, test usable would be 4 / 7 and train 12 / 27. Test episodes that fail on a gap inside the push: ep_016 (2.5 s),
ep_027 (2.4 s), ep_031 (1.35 s).

Why the gaps are long: looking at frames the detector missed in ep_026 (34% coverage; ep_025 has 20%; their longest
gaps are 4 s in a 12-15 s window), the hand is out of the frame or the finger is not visible while the box sits at the
image edge, so those frames contain no fingertip to detect. Lower confidence and cropping raised coverage from 47% to
72% on the train subset; I did not check the other episodes' missed frames one by one.
